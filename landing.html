What's inside

Everything a study day needs, and nothing it doesn't.

Your sessions, settings and subjects live on your account, so the timer you set on a laptop is the one waiting on your phone.

Timer or stopwatch

A Pomodoro countdown or a count-up stopwatch. Starting either hides everything else for a clean screen — and pausing logs your time, so a half-finished session is never lost.

Study rooms

Make a room public so anyone can find it, or invite-only with a share link. A live leaderboard shows everyone's focus time and who is studying right now.

Work you're given

Whoever runs a room can hand out a task with a deadline and a suggested length. It lands in your timer's task panel with a “Let's do it” button that sets the clock for you.

Calendar & streaks

Every finished session records its minutes and its subject. The dashboard shows a month at a glance, a 14-day chart and your streak — tap any day to see exactly what you studied.

Your own subjects

Make the subjects you actually study, pick one before you start, and each shows the time you've put into it today right next to its name.

A library that fits you

Tell us your grade and major when you sign up, and your shelf holds the material published for exactly that — organised into categories, open or downloadable.

Make it yours

Choose a background — a gradient or your own photo — dim it, blur it, pick an accent, and switch between the classic and glass looks. Whatever you choose follows you to every page.

English & فارسی

The whole app speaks both, right-to-left included — the timer, the dashboard, the rooms and the library. Switch language any time; nothing you've saved changes.

The Android app

It keeps counting after you put the phone down.

The same app, wrapped in a native shell — plus four things only a phone can do.

Download the APKAndroid 8 and up
01
A timer in the notification bar

The session runs as a real foreground service, so it keeps counting with the app closed — or swiped away. Pause, resume and end it from the shade.

02
App lock

Optional, and off by default. While a focus block runs, opening an app you didn't allow covers it with a reminder and a way straight back.

03
A real “time's up”

The end-of-block alert is scheduled natively, so it arrives even if Android had put the app to sleep long before.

04
Library downloads

Files from your shelf go through Android's own download manager — a progress notification, a retry, and a tap to open, exactly like any other download.
