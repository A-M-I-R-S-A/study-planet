About Study Planet

Built by someone who needed it.

One person, one Pomodoro timer that kept growing — accounts, study rooms, a library, an Android app — each piece added because it was the next thing missing, not because a roadmap said so.

How it started

A Pomodoro timer that kept growing.

Every piece of Study Planet exists because something about studying with borrowed, scattered tools stopped working.

Started with a timer

A Pomodoro countdown, a live clock, ambient sound — the parts an actual study session needs, not what a template happened to include.

Grew by need, not by plan

Accounts came so settings would follow between devices. Rooms came so studying didn't have to be solitary. The library came because material still had to be found somewhere else.

Still one person

No team, no roadmap meetings — someone who studies with the same app everyone else does, fixing what actually gets in the way.

Who's behind it

One person, building it in his own time.
